Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers Meow Studio Publisher, an internal application that Meow Studio uses to publish videos to its own YouTube channel.

Who uses the app

Only the Meow Studio team, signed in with the Google account that owns the Meow Studio channel. The app is not offered to the public.

Google data we access

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos that we created to our own channel.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to read our channel's name, video list and video status.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read statistics of our own videos (views, average watch time, shares, new subscribers).

How we use it

Only to publish our own videos and to measure how they perform, so we can decide what to make next. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone. We do not use it to train general AI models.

Storage and security

The access token and the statistics are stored on the studio's own computer, in a private folder that only the studio can read. Nothing is stored in a public place.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept as long as the studio needs them to compare its videos. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer read or upload anything, and the stored token stops working.

Compliance

Meow Studio Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads also follow the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
