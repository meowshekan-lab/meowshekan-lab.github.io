Short, wordless AI videos

Meow Studio is a small creative studio that makes short vertical videos with generative AI tools: visual surprises, transformations, satisfying motion and tiny mini-dramas that anyone can enjoy without words.

What the Meow Studio Publisher app does

Meow Studio Publisher is our own internal tool. It uploads the videos we make to our own YouTube channel and reads that channel's own statistics (views, watch time, shares) so we can learn which videos people enjoy.

It is used only by the Meow Studio team for our own channel. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access anyone else's channel or data.

Every video is labeled as AI-generated on the platform where it is published.
